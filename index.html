Help

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose my size?

Women's sandals come in sizes 36 to 41 and men's sandals in sizes 39 to 45 (EU sizing). If you are between two sizes, we recommend the larger one.

How long does delivery take?

Accra usually takes 1 to 2 working days. Other regions usually take 2 to 5 working days. Free delivery is available on orders over GH₵ 300.

Can I return or exchange my sandals?

Yes. You can return or exchange unworn sandals in their original packaging within 7 days of delivery.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Mobile Money, card payments and pay on delivery in Accra. In this demo, no real payment is taken.

How will I know my order is confirmed?

After you place your order, we send you the details on WhatsApp. Our team will confirm your order and delivery time.
